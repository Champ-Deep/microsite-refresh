The same coverage, readable without the globe

Everything the hero draws also lives in plain HTML: the counters above, the refresh date and the coverage table beside this text. The globe is an upgrade for people, never a requirement.

  • Poster first. The planet and every word of copy paint before the globe script runs. The scene loads after the page is interactive.
  • One JSON. Country, contacts, companies, industry split and top titles. Nightly export, no backend.
  • Conversion wired in. Hovering a market rewrites the call to action and prefills the sample form with that market and industry.
  • Events for the A/B test. globe_loaded, country_hover, country_click, industry_change and sample_cta_click log to the console here and go to GA4 in production.
