# Prospecting one-page design

## 1. Visual theme and atmosphere
A company introduction, not a catalog funnel. Calm editorial layout with an open split overview and ruled services. Five visible sections, no badges, invented proof or decorative scenes.
## 2. Color palette and roles
Champions fixture: white #FFFFFF, ink #1A1A1A, peach #FFF4EC, small accent text #A8400C, muted #565656, rule #DEDBD7, contact ground #1C1210 with explicit #FFF4EC text. Use the prospect's approved brand tokens when adapting a real site.
## 3. Typography rules
Inter, inherited from the Champions brand. Self-hosted from the existing plugin font. Body 16px, overview 18 to 22px, heading 38 to 76px, section 28 to 42px. Body measure at most 65ch.
## 4. Component stylings
21st.dev 19078 split title/description is ported to vanilla; no demo cards or fake social proof. Existing host anchor navigation and hairline rows. Text-link hover and visible 3px focus ring. Email is a real mailto link.
## 5. Layout principles
Fluid 20 to 80px gutters, 1600px maximum. Balanced split columns on desktop, single column below 700px. No horizontal cards, nested panels or modal.
## 6. Depth and elevation
Flat paper and hairlines. Dark contact section is a hierarchy change, not an overlay. No shadows or glass.
## 7. Do and do not
Show overview, services, contact, about and privacy in the initial HTML. Do not add CTA variants, source buttons, audio, badge bubbles, unsupported claims or legal assurances.
## 8. Responsive behavior
Pointer and touch web. Validate 375, 768, 1440. All links at least 44px high. Mobile header wraps deliberately. No sticky surfaces. Motion budget: one 200ms arrow hover; reduced motion disables it and smooth scrolling. No JavaScript.
## 9. Agent prompt guide
Preserve this sibling's contract and all incumbent templates. Retheme only with owner-approved tokens and validate contrast. Refuse incomplete inputs. The worked example stays noindex until real copy and privacy terms are approved in a separate release.
