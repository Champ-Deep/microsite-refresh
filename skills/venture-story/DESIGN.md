# Venture Story: Champions Group

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
An editorial corporate homepage with an architectural motion narrative. YuVerse is the behavioral reference, not a source of copy or assets. White space, oversized type, warm orange engineering diagrams and a single warm-ink proof section. No generic SaaS dashboards, invented live feeds or floating KPI tiles.

Audience: founders, prospective business partners and enterprise buyers. Belief: the group offers complementary capabilities, rather than a disconnected directory. Primary action: start a conversation with the group. Strongest proof: the named ventures and Lake B2B's attributed scale. This is a local design concept, not an authorized production redesign.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
Champions brand tokens take precedence. Ground #FFFFFF, warm section #FFF4EC, text #1A1A1A, secondary #62605E, rule #E5E1DC, accent #F26722, bright #FF8544, dark #1C1210. Orange is geometry and large display accents, not small body text. Small accent text and filled action buttons use #A83B08, a contrast-safe derivative. White text explicitly set on dark sections. The supplied logo is not recolored.

## 3. Typography Rules
Self-hosted Inter variable, with system fallback. Hero 48 to 92px, weight 500, tracking -0.065em. Section headlines 36 to 64px, weight 500, tracking -0.05em. Body 16 to 18px, line height 1.65. Eyebrows 11px, uppercase, tracking .13em. No gradient-clipped text. No mono UI shorthand.

## 4. Component Stylings
Small rounded rectangle CTAs with inset arrow affordance. Visible 2px focus ring and 48px targets. Compact floating navigation, opaque fallback, functional mobile disclosure. Directory is a filterable editorial list, not a wall of cards. Native details expose venture descriptions without JavaScript. Filter buttons retain keyboard focus. The hero architectural scene is decorative and has a DOM equivalent.

## 5. Layout Principles
Max content width 1280px; desktop gutters 56px, mobile 22px. Asymmetric hero 0.95fr/1.05fr. Tall chapter stage with legible left copy and visual right story. Ordinary-flow version is the baseline. No pinning on short screens or below 1024px. No fake scrollbars, scroll hijacking or forced snap. Native scroll remains in control.

## 6. Depth & Elevation
An isometric four-layer architectural illustration represents the four ecosystems. Local SVG geometry with material gradients and fine edges, not a new logo. Only the nav uses a small shadow. Illustration plates have physical extrusion. No decorative glass cards. Ring, line and node animations explain connection or sequence.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts
Keep business copy and venture facts in site.json. Derive group and venture counts. Attribute database scale to Lake B2B, never to group clients or revenue. No real-time indicator, fabricated clients, performance claims, or contact forms that fake submission. No copied YuVerse code, logos or customer names. No invented office addresses. Keep all chapter copy in raw HTML. Do not repurpose the existing verify-skin or change its default.

## 8. Responsive Behavior
Device class: pointer and touch marketing page. 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 widths plus short-landscape covered. Desktop GSAP ScrollTrigger pins a connected-venture chapter stage and scrubs visual states. Phones receive flowing chapters with lightweight entrance motion. Reduced motion, no JS and missing GSAP show all final-state content. Changes to reduced-motion preference revert pinning live. Motion budget: bundled JS under 300KB uncompressed, no WebGL, no continuous render loop, aim 60fps; hardware GPU performance must be reported unverified without measurement. One time-based intro, all major scene transitions driven by actual scroll. Self-contained preview has no external requests.

## 9. Agent Prompt Guide
Choose this opt-in template for a multi-venture group or multi-product B2B homepage where the relationships matter. Preserve structure and motion, swap the JSON copy, four ecosystem entries, brand tokens and a local logo. GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger drive the main narrative. Existing LakeKit supplies micro-interactions. Build with Python standard library, no Node install. Verify both sample content and alternate content build. Deliver artifact.html and the handoff folder. Do not publish or commit without authorization.
